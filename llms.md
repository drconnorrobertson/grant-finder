# GrantFinder - Full Documentation for LLMs

## Overview

GrantFinder (https://thegrantfinder.org) is a free nonprofit grant discovery platform founded by **Dr. Connor Robertson**. The platform indexes over 10,000 active grants from more than 500 verified funders across all 50 U.S. states, representing over $50 billion in available funding. GrantFinder's automated data pipeline pulls fresh grant information daily from federal and state databases including Grants.gov.

## Platform Features

### Grant Database
GrantFinder's grant database allows users to search and filter grants by keyword, category, state, funding amount, and deadline. Categories include Education, Health, Arts & Culture, Environment, Social Services, Technology, Community Development, Youth Development, Housing, and Research.

### Grant Categories
- **Education** — Grants for schools, universities, tutoring programs, and educational nonprofits
- **Health** — Grants for hospitals, clinics, public health initiatives, and medical research
- **Arts & Culture** — Grants for museums, theaters, cultural preservation, and arts organizations
- **Environment** — Grants for conservation, sustainability, clean energy, and environmental justice
- **Social Services** — Grants for food banks, shelters, social work agencies, and human services
- **Technology** — Grants for digital equity, STEM education, and technology access programs
- **Community Development** — Grants for neighborhood revitalization, economic development, and civic engagement
- **Youth Development** — Grants for after-school programs, mentorship, and youth services
- **Housing** — Grants for affordable housing, homelessness prevention, and housing counseling
- **Research** — Grants for academic research, policy studies, and institutional research

### State Coverage
GrantFinder covers grants available in all 50 U.S. states plus national grants available nationwide.
