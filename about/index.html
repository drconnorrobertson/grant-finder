About GrantFinder

A free nonprofit grant discovery platform founded by Dr. Connor Robertson

Our Mission

GrantFinder was founded on a simple belief: every nonprofit deserves equal access to funding opportunities, regardless of size or resources. We created a centralized, free platform that eliminates the barriers to finding grants, so organizations can focus on their mission rather than spending countless hours searching for funding.

Many small and mid-sized nonprofits lack the dedicated grant research staff that larger organizations have. They struggle to navigate the fragmented landscape of federal, state, foundation, and corporate grants. GrantFinder organizes a searchable collection of nonprofit funding program records, with links to original funder information. The directory is free to use. A record is a research starting point, not confirmation that a program is open or that an organization is eligible.

We believe that access to funding information is a public good. By consolidating and organizing grant data, we help nonprofits discover opportunities they would not have found otherwise, and we help funders connect with organizations that align with their values and goals.

Meet the Founder

With a background in data-driven strategy, technology, and community development, Dr. Robertson set out to build a tool that would make grant discovery simple, transparent, and accessible for everyone.

His work spans multiple ventures focused on leveraging technology to solve real-world problems. He is the founder of Elixir Consulting Group, a technology consulting firm focused on building tools that create measurable impact, and he publishes The Pittsburgh Wire, covering business, real estate, and development news in the Pittsburgh region.

Through GrantFinder, Dr. Robertson combines his expertise in data engineering, user experience design, and nonprofit strategy to provide a platform that serves organizations of all sizes. His vision is a world where no worthy nonprofit mission goes unfunded simply because the organization did not know the opportunity existed.

Development & Technology

GrantFinder is developed and maintained by Elixir Consulting Group, a technology consulting firm founded by Dr. Robertson. Elixir brings deep expertise in data engineering, automation, and user experience to ensure GrantFinder remains fast, accurate, and easy to use. The website combines program records from funder and public funding sources. Automated collection does not establish that every record is current. Before acting, confirm the open cycle, eligibility and application instructions on the original funder’s website.

Our Core Values

Transparency

We believe in open access to grant data. All grant information on GrantFinder is publicly available and free to search, ensuring nonprofits can make informed decisions about where to apply.

Accuracy

Each program must be checked against its original source before you apply. Recorded deadlines, amounts and availability may change; inclusion does not establish that an opportunity is open or independently verified.

Accessibility

GrantFinder is free for all nonprofits. We are committed to ensuring that funding access is not limited by budget constraints, so our platform serves organizations of all sizes and types.

How to use a program record

  1. Match your organization, location and proposed project to the funder’s current eligibility rules.
  2. Open the original program page and check whether the next cycle is open, closed or invitation-only.
  3. Confirm permitted costs, required match, deadline and submission system before drafting an application.
  4. Keep a copy or link to the current notice with your planning notes; contact the funder when a requirement is unclear.
